# A Journey of Faith

A Full HD video of about 19 minutes, with Scripture and gentle music. Two source clips over 100 MB could not be downloaded and are not included. The precise location of every shot has not been independently verified.

## Download as one MP4

Download `Download_Journey.html` using GitHub’s **Download raw file** button. Open the downloaded file in Chrome or Edge, then click **Download the video**. It downloads and verifies each part, then saves `A_Journey_of_Faith.mp4`. Keep the page open until it finishes. The download is about 925 MB. Phones may need a computer instead.

Alternatively, download `download_journey.py` and run `python download_journey.py`. It saves one verified MP4. Python 3 is required.

Video SHA256: `e28d64890fc87a878dd94bcbd728976b0d23708515c96da4496032ee67b67b29`.

## Save directly to Google Drive

Open `Save_to_Google_Drive.ipynb` in Google Colab. Press the play button and connect the Google account where you want the video saved. It downloads, combines, verifies, and saves the video in My Drive inside the Abbas Table folder. Allow about 1 GB of free Drive space. Keep the notebook open until it finishes.
